There is one way to settle the "who keeps logs" argument without trusting anyone's marketing: give law enforcement a warrant and watch what happens. In April 2023, Swedish police walked into Mullvad's office with a search warrant. They left with zero bytes of user data. No connection logs, no IPs, nothing to hand over.

That real-world test is the frame for this comparison. A VPN for crypto users has three jobs: hide your traffic from your ISP, resist revealing you even under legal compulsion, and accept payment methods that don't link your identity to your subscription. Every provider below is evaluated on those three criteria — not on server counts or Netflix compatibility.

Mullvad — the most anonymous option

Mullvad doesn't ask for an email address. You get a randomly generated 16-digit account number and that's it. You pay with cash mailed to Sweden, cryptocurrency (10% discount), or a normal card — and if you pay cash or crypto, the company cannot associate the account with you even if compelled.

  • Audits: 8+ including Cure53, X41, NCC Group, Assured. The 2023 police raid is the definitive no-logs proof.
  • Price: €5/month flat since 2009. No promos, no renewal hikes, no tiers.
  • Speed: WireGuard-only since January 2026; ~700 servers; no streaming support by design.
  • Verdict: The gold standard if your threat model includes "I don't want to be identified as a VPN customer at all."

Proton VPN — privacy architecture with a live ecosystem

Founded by CERN scientists, based in Switzerland (outside the 14-Eyes surveillance alliance), non-profit foundation owned. Open-source apps, five consecutive annual Securitum no-logs audits (2022–2026) plus SOC 2 Type II.

  • Signup: Email required (use a Proton Mail address via their free tier to keep it in-house — or any throwaway).
  • Payment: Cards, PayPal, Bitcoin, cash resellers.
  • Secure Core: double-hop routing through Proton-controlled servers — the strongest architecture against exit-server compromise, at a real speed cost.
  • Price: Free tier (unlimited data, no ads) or ~$3.99–10/mo paid; 30-day money-back.
  • Verdict: Best balanced pick for crypto users who also want Proton Mail / Drive / Pass under one Swiss roof.

NordVPN — most audited, fastest, most popular

Six independent no-logs audits since 2018 by PwC and Deloitte, one of the most extensive audit records in the industry. Fast (NordLynx, WireGuard-based), 8,400+ RAM-only servers, reliable streaming.

  • Signup: Email mandatory.
  • Payment: Cards and crypto (Bitcoin on some plans).
  • Caveats: Windows/macOS/iOS apps closed-source (Linux client open-sourced Oct 2025); 2018 server breach publicly handled; complex ownership ($3B valuation, majority non-founder-owned).
  • Price: ~$2.99–3.09/mo on 2-year deals, renews higher; $12.99/mo monthly.
  • Verdict: The right default if daily usability and streaming matter more than signup anonymity.

Why most "best VPN" lists are lying to you

NordVPN pays affiliates up to 100% commission on signups. ExpressVPN too. VPN reviews are among the most commission-driven content on the internet — a $30–60 first-sale payout means providers dominate rankings by commission rate, not privacy. Mullvad pays $0 and is excluded from most lists for that exact reason. We rank deliberately: money is not the ranking factor; privacy is.

Our recommendation for crypto users

  1. Pure anonymity is the goal: Mullvad. No email, cash/crypto payment, raid-proven. Nothing matches it.
  2. You want privacy plus a whole Swiss ecosystem: Proton VPN. Open source, audited, and you can run your mail through Proton too.
  3. You need speed and streaming with a strong audit record: NordVPN — most audited providers at consumer scale.
