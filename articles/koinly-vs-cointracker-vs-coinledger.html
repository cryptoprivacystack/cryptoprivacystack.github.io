The 2025 cost basis rule nobody's ready for

Under Rev. Proc. 2024-28, cost basis must be tracked wallet-by-wallet starting January 1, 2025 — the old account-level pooling produces phantom gains and zeroed-out basis on moving coins. All three tools now support per-wallet basis, but you must verify the setting is actually applied in your account: accounts created under the old method can silently produce wrong numbers.

What no software fully automates

Cross-chain bridges, liquid staking derivatives and restaking still produce errors in all three products. A wallet-to-wallet transfer mislabeled as a sale creates a phantom gain on one side and resets basis to zero on the other. Budget a manual review pass on DeFi-heavy years — or have a crypto CPA review the output before filing.

Bottom line

  1. Under ~1,000 transactions: Koinly ($49-99) or CoinLedger ($49-99) — pick Koinly for international, CoinLedger for US simplicity.
  2. Coinbase-heavy US filer: CoinTracker, for the TurboTax path and portfolio UI.
  3. High volume / DeFi: CoinLedger's flat $299 wins at scale.
  4. Non-US: Koinly, no contest on 100+ country reporting.

Software gets you 80–90% of the way. The last 10–20% — 1099-DA mismatches, bridges, staking edge cases — is where the expensive errors hide.
